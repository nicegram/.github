# Nicegram

**Nicegram** is an **AI-first, privacy-focused operating system for productivity built on top of Telegram**.

Telegram is the world’s best **communication transport layer** (fast, global, scalable).  
Nicegram OS adds the missing **OS layer**: **Structure + Memory + Execution** — without forcing users or teams to migrate away from Telegram.

## Core thesis

**Business has moved to Telegram for external communication (clients, partners, deals).  
The tools haven’t.**  
Nicegram OS becomes the default **work layer** for professionals and teams who already operate in Telegram.

## What Nicegram is

An **alternative Telegram client** for running your work accounts — with the features the official app does not have, and none of the friction of leaving it.

- **Sign in your business accounts and manage them.** Several at once, in one place, without juggling devices.
- **Bring AI to your Telegram.** Read, draft, summarise and act on what is already in your chats.
- **Open source.** Every client below is published in this organisation. You can read what runs on your device, build it yourself, and check that the two match.
- **Privacy first.** Your account stays on Telegram’s servers under Telegram’s terms — we do not stand between you and them, and we add no tracking of our own. Being open source is what makes that claim checkable rather than a promise.
- **Built on Telegram’s own source, kept in sync.** Each client is a fork of the official app, merged forward as Telegram ships. Updates arrive, and the interface stays the one you already know.
- **Community.** Built in the open, with the people who use it.

**60+ million installs** across our clients.
